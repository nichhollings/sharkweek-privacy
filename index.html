SharkWeek Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

SharkWeek is a period tracking app for iPhone. It is built so that your information stays on your device. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

What the app stores

You can record period start and end dates and write free-form notes for periods and individual days. This information is stored only in the app’s local storage on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. SharkWeek has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app makes no network requests. The developer cannot see, access or receive any of your data.

Sharing and exports

The Reports screen lets you export a PDF, a CSV spreadsheet or a text summary. Exports are created on your device and leave it only if you choose to share them, for example by sending them to your doctor. After that, the app or person you share them with is responsible for them.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, the app’s data may be included in that backup, as with any app. Those backups are controlled by Apple and by you.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual periods and notes in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Health information

Predictions of future periods, fertile windows and ovulation are estimates based on the dates you enter. They are not medical advice and must not be used for contraception.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

For help using the app, see the support page. Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/nichhollings/sharkweek-privacy/issues.
