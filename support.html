SharkWeek Support

A simple, private period tracker for iPhone.

Logging a period that’s happening now

On the Calendar tab, tap Start Period Today. When it finishes, tap End Period Today.

Entering past periods

Tap Edit on the calendar, then tap the first day of a period and then its last day. The days in between fill in. Tap a marked day to unmark it. Use the arrows to move between months, and tap Done when finished. You can also add a past period with the + button on the History tab.

Adding notes

Tap any day on the calendar to write a note for that day. To add notes to a whole period, open the period from the History tab.

Predictions

Predicted periods use the average of your last six cycles (28 days until you’ve logged at least two periods). The fertile window and ovulation day are calendar estimates only. They are not medical advice and must not be used for contraception.

Exporting for your doctor

On the Reports tab, choose a date range, then share a PDF report, a CSV spreadsheet or a text summary.

Your data

Everything is stored on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes its data. See the privacy policy.
